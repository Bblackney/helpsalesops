⚡ Automation Opportunity
Merges and MRR syncs are the top 2 request types across every month. Both are rule-based and repetitive. Merge bot is already partially there. MRR sync self-service or automated trigger would eliminate the highest-volume ticket type.
📚 Training Signal
~30% of volume is "how does this work" questions that appear month after month from different reps. A searchable KB, updated Seismic articles, or a #sales-ops-tips channel would deflect this without any systems work.
⚖️ ROE Clarity Gap
BDR credit on card-ins and strat reps holding velocity accounts are structurally recurring — not edge cases. Both require a system-level fix: automated BDR attribution logic and tighter routing rules, not manual adjudication.
📅 EOM Surge Risk
Discount/PW approvals spike 2–3× at EOM with "client on the line" urgency consistently across every month. Extended approver coverage or an automated approval queue would reduce SLA risk significantly.
🔀 Integration Watch — July 2026
Dupe merges are already the #1 request type all year. Post Themis/Core integration (July 2026), expect another sustained spike. Proactive dupe detection, merge queue automation, and rep comms should be scoped now.
🔧 Tool Release Cluster Risk
Both ChiliPiper and Salesloft issues cluster around their respective release windows — each incident generates 15–30+ individual requests in a short burst. Pre-release comms and a posted workaround would reduce noise significantly.
