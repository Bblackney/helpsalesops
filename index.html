Key Observations
Automation Opportunity
Merge requests are already partially automated (bot confirms receipt). MRR sync, discount approvals, and opp re-opens are high-frequency and rule-based — strong candidates for further automation or self-service flows.
Training Signal
~30% of volume is questions about how things work — SFDC logic, segment rules, routing. This is preventable. A searchable knowledge base or updated Seismic content would deflect a meaningful portion of this bucket.
ROE Clarity Gap
ROE disputes cluster around BDR credit on inbound card-ins and strat reps holding velocity accounts. Both are structural — indicate routing logic gaps or unclear policy at the rep level, not one-off judgment calls.
EOM Volume Spike
Discount / PW approval requests spike 2–3x at EOM, often with "client on the line" urgency. This creates SLA risk. An approval queue or extended approver coverage at EOM would reduce escalation pressure.
Integration Noise
Dupe account merges remain the #1 single request type. Post Themis/Core integration (July 2026), expect this to spike again. Proactive merge queue monitoring and automated dupe detection should be prioritized now.
Salesloft Cluster Risk
Salesloft issues (local dial, logging failures, sync errors) tend to cluster around release windows. Each incident generates 10–20+ individual help requests. Proactive comms at release time would reduce noise.
Note: Volume estimates based on representative sampling (~300 parent messages) across Jan–Jun 2026. Percentages are directional, not exact counts. Bot messages (Merge Request confirmations, Email Release confirmations) excluded from categorization.
